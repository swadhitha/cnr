## Scenarios

- **stable** (legitimate): Legitimate PU only, stationary channel.
- **power_drift** (legitimate): PU transmit power ramps +6 dB (x4) between slots 400 and 1400.
- **shadowing_drift** (legitimate): Shadowing std ramps 4.0 -> 5.5 dB between slots 400 and 1400.
- **noise_drift** (legitimate): Noise floor rises +4 dB between slots 400 and 1400.
- **combined_drift** (legitimate): Power +4 dB, shadowing +1 dB, noise +3 dB, all ramped between slots 400 and 1400.
- **basic_puea** (attack): Attacker at basic_xy with basic_power_dbm transmits in 30% of slots after slot 500.
- **power_matching_puea** (attack): Attacker at basic_xy chooses its power so its mean RSS over the SUs equals the PU's (assumes it knows SU positions and alpha).
- **gradual_puea** (attack): Open-loop evasion: PU-likeness q steps through the stages; q moves the attacker from basic_xy toward a point min_distance_to_pu from the PU and moves its power from basic_power_dbm to the matched power.
- **stealthy_puea** (attack): Adaptive attacker placed distance_from_pu from the PU that tracks the PU's (drifting) power every slot and transmits rarely. PU has a +6 dB power drift.
- **poisoning** (attack): Closed-loop boiling-frog poisoning. Phase 1 (slots < poison_start): PU only. Phase 2: attacker injects poison in poison_rate of slots with progress p in [0,1] interpolating from a PU-mimic (start_distance_from_pu from the PU, power-matched) to the target (basic_xy, basic_power_dbm); p increases by step_up when the slot was NOT flagged (SUs vacated) and decreases by step_down when flagged. Phase 3 (slots >= attack_start): attacker transmits at the target in attack_rate of slots.
- **near_pu_poisoning** (attack): Closed-loop poisoning inside the anchor's resolution limit: poison starts 3 m from the PU and the target is 8 m from the PU (toward basic_xy) at matched power + 8 dB.
- **slow_near_pu_poisoning** (attack): Low-duty-cycle version of near_pu_poisoning: poison injected in only 5% of slots (minimises the sequential test's evidence per slot), same start/target points and attack phase.
